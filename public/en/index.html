Fair questions. Clear answers.

Request a site
Do I have to buy the demo website?

No. The preview lets you evaluate the proposal. If it isn’t right for your business, you can decline.

How much does a website cost?

The three packages cost $199, $249 and $350. Check the packages above to see what each one includes. Extra requirements and recurring costs are agreed before purchase.

Can I ask for changes?

Yes. We discuss what you’d like to adjust and agree together on the changes included in the offer before it’s finalized.

What about the domain and hosting?

We set out in the offer how the website will be published, who manages the domain and hosting, what access you get and what upkeep costs.

Can I contact you if I haven’t received a proposal?

Of course. Tell us about your business, the services you offer and what you want from a website. We take it from there.
