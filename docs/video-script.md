# Video message script (Romanian, about 30 seconds)

For the founder block on the page and for outreach. She records it herself, on her phone.

## Script (RO)

Bună! Sunt [Prenume], din București.

Știu ce gândești când primești un mesaj despre un site nou: „Oare nu e o țeapă?”

Așa că fac invers. Fac mai întâi site-ul tău. Complet: cu numele afacerii, cu culorile și textele tale. Apoi ți-l arăt.

Nu plătești nimic până nu vezi cum arată. Dacă nu-ți place, nu datorezi nimic.

Scrie-mi pe WhatsApp numele afacerii tale și începem.

Mulțumesc că m-ai ascultat. Te aștept!

## Meaning (EN)

Hi! I'm [Name], from Bucharest. I know what you think when you get a message about a new website: "Isn't this a scam?" So I do it the other way around. First I build your website. Complete: with your business name, your colors and your texts. Then I show it to you. You pay nothing until you see how it looks. If you don't like it, you owe nothing. Message me your business name on WhatsApp and we start. Thank you for listening. I'm waiting for you!

## Notes

- *Țeapă* is slang for getting ripped off. It is how people really say it. *Înșelătorie* is the cleaner word if she prefers it.
- It must be **really her**: phone camera, her own face and voice. No AI avatar. A skeptical viewer spots it fast and it defeats the purpose.
- Vertical (9:16), window light, phone at eye level, her own words (not read stiffly).
- Burned-in Romanian subtitles, because many people watch muted.
- File under 10 MB (mp4).
- Do not say the phone number in the video. It may change. The page shows it under the video.
- Say only what is true. "You owe nothing" must match the real terms.
- The transcript on the page must match what she actually says. If she changes words, change the transcript too.
